Assessment

In this project, different assessment tools will be used.
They will help to evaluate:

  • the final video (Final product rubric)
  • the group work process (Checklist for group work)
  • and your reflection about what you have learned (Self-assessment and final reflection)

You will know in advance what is going to be assessed and how it will be assessed.

1. Final product rubric

This rubric will be used to assess the final video. Some aspects will be assessed as a group and others individually.
