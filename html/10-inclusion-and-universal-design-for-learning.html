Inclusion and Universal Design for Learning

This project has been designed to be accessible, flexible and inclusive for all students.

Different support measures are included during the project:

  • activation of prior knowledge
  • visual support and clear instructions
  • use of simple and guided digital tools
  • flexible grouping
  • teamwork and peer support
  • different ways of participating in the final product.

The project also follows some basic principles of Universal Design for Learning (UDL):

  • multiple means of representation, through videos, texts, visuals and digital resources
  • multiple means of action and expression, through speaking, writing, planning and creating a video
  • multiple means of engagement, through a meaningful topic, collaborative work and a motivating final product.

These measures aim to help all students participate in the project according to their needs and abilities.

Support measures in this project

  • Clear explanations before each task
  • Visual and written support for instructions
  • Guided use of Padlet, Google Classroom and Canva
  • Flexible grouping in pairs and small groups
  • Peer support during digital tasks
  • Possibility to contribute in different ways to the final video
  • Extra help when technical difficulties appear
  • Focus on participation, progress and communication
