Healthy Habits FAQ Video

3rd ESO English Project about health and healthy lifestyles

3. FINAL PRODUCT

Healthy Habits FAQ Video

What is the final product?
The final product of this project is a video in English about healthy habits.

What do you have to do?
In small groups, you will create a video with frequently asked questions (FAQs) and answers about health and healthy habits.

Your video can include questions and answers about:

  • healthy food
  • exercise
  • sleep
  • daily routines
  • unhealthy habits

How will you work?
You will work in small groups.
Each group will:

  • choose some questions
  • write short answers in English
  • prepare the video
  • and present it to the class

What digital tool will you use?
You will use Canva to plan and create your video.

Your video should be clear, simple and useful for other students!!
